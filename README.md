> **"Flow is the state in which people are so involved in an activity that nothing else seems to matter."** — *Mihaly Csikszentmihalyi*

FlowState is a minimalist, privacy-first productivity tool designed for those who find traditional Pomodoro timers too restrictive. It replaces forced interruptions with **Progressive Flow Tracking**, allowing your focus to dictate the duration of your work, not an arbitrary buzzer.

---

## 🧠 The Philosophy: Why "Progressive"?

Standard Pomodoro (25m work / 5m break) was designed for repetitive tasks. For creative and technical professionals—developers, writers, and designers—the 25-minute buzzer often hits just as you enter **"The Zone."** Forced context switching at this peak moment is a productivity killer.

### The Problem with 25/5:
1. **The Context Switching Tax:** It can take 15–23 minutes to get back into deep focus after an interruption.
2. **Anxiety-Driven Focus:** Watching a clock count down to zero creates a "scarcity" mindset.
3. **Flow Interruption:** It treats all work blocks as equal, ignoring the reality of varying task complexity.

### The FlowState Solution:
FlowState uses **Progressive Tracking**. You don't set a timer; you **start a session**.
- **Ride the Wave:** If you're in the zone for 90 minutes, stay there.
- **Intentional Breaks:** Move to "Rest" mode only when your natural cognitive energy dips.
- **Distraction Mapping:** Specifically log when and why your focus broke to build self-awareness.

## ✨ Key Features

### 1. Intent-Driven Sessions
Before every session, you must "Declare your Mission." This simple psychological hurdle ensures you aren't just "being busy," but are working with a specific objective.

### 2. The Visual Flow Timeline
A beautiful, high-fidelity canvas visualization of your day. It isn't just a list; it’s a heartbeat monitor of your productivity.
- **Emerald:** Deep, uninterrupted focus.
- **Sky:** Rejuvenating rest.
- **Rose:** Moments where focus was compromised (distractions).
- **The Void:** Automatically tracks gaps between sessions as "Unlogged Time."

### 3. ADHD & Neurodivergent Friendly
Many neurodivergent minds struggle with rigid time-boxes (Time Blindness). FlowState accommodates this by:
- Providing a "Stopwatch" feel rather than a "Countdown."
- Allowing for instant mode-switching.
- Requiring a "Reflection" note for distractions to help identify patterns of executive dysfunction.

### 4. Privacy by Design
- **100% Local:** Your data never leaves your browser. No accounts, no clouds, no tracking.
- **CSV Export:** You own your data. Export your full history for personal analysis or billing.
- **PWA Ready:** Install it as a standalone app on your desktop or mobile device.

## 🚀 Getting Started

### Installation
FlowState is a **Progressive Web App (PWA)**. 
1. Visit the [hosted URL](https://uttamjodawat.github.io/flowstate/).
2. On Chrome: Click the "Install" icon in the address bar.
3. On iOS: Tap "Share" and "Add to Home Screen."

### Usage
1. **Declare:** Type your mission (e.g., "Refactor Auth Logic").
2. **Flow:** Hit `Focus`. Work until you feel a dip in energy.
3. **Rest:** Hit `Rest`. Take as long as you need to feel refreshed.
4. **Reflect:** If a notification or colleague distracts you, hit `Distracted`. Record what happened.
5. **Analyze:** At the end of the block, review your **Flow Metrics** to see your average deep work endurance.
